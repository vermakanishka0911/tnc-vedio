# TNC Nursing — Android Application

Native Android app built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose for TNC Nursing Classes (India's premier nursing exam preparation platform for NORCET, AIIMS, CHO, and ESIC).

## Features

- **Home Dashboard**: Study streak tracker, study hours & lecture progress summary, promotional highlight banners, quick navigation.
- **Courses Catalog**: Full searchable catalog with favorites bookmarking and category filtering.
- **Subjects & Curriculum**: Detailed subject breakdown with lecture and e-notes counts.
- **Lesson Player**: Full video lecture playback supporting YouTube embeds, Firebase secured video streams, and direct media streams with heartbeat study time tracking and "Mark Complete" progress tracking.
- **E-Notes & PDFs**: In-app PDF lecture notes viewer with external viewer fallback.
- **Watched & Learning History**: Recent watch history with quick resume and completed lectures list.
- **Leaderboard**: Real-time study ranking and medal podium for top nursing exam aspirants.
- **Test Series Integration**: Direct launcher for NORCET mock test CBT exams.
- **Theme & Identity**: Seamless Light (warm paper) and Dark themes with customizable learner profile.

## Tech Stack

- **UI**: Jetpack Compose & Material Design 3 (M3)
- **Language**: Kotlin 2.2.10
- **Build System**: Gradle 9.3.1 with Android Gradle Plugin 9.1.1
- **Networking**: Retrofit 2 + OkHttp + Gson
- **Image Loading**: Coil Compose
- **Target SDK**: Android 36 (Android 16)
